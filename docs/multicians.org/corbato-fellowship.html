At the 2004 Multics Reunion on June 19, 2004, we announced that we have established a new graduate student fellowship in honor of Professor Fernando J. Corbató. It's our way of saying "thanks" to him for the education that we all received under his leadership.

We are gratified by the response from many Multicians who have pledged to this fund. If you have not yet responded, would you please join with us in making a pledge to support this fund? Your gift will ensure that Corby's legacy of research and education lives on in the next generation of MIT students.

Fernando J. Corbató (1956) Fellowship Fund
