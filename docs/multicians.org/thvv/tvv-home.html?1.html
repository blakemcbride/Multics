Multics

I worked on the Multics operating system for almost 16 years in the 60s and 70s, and edit a web site describing the system, its history, and its builders.

  • "Phase One"
    Noel Morris and I combined all the pieces of Multics, ran a cold system boot, and executed commands on the GE-645 for the first time in December 1967.

    "Software Development Process"
    I was a founding member of the Multics Change Review Board that defined our development process. I established, documented, and taught our peer code review process.

    "New Storage System"
    I coordinated a team of 10-15 people in 1973-76 doing the implementation and rollout of a rewriten Multics file system, providing better capacity, robustness, and performance.

    "The Multics System Programming Process"
    Charlie Clingen and I wrote an invited paper (06/78) for COMPCON 78 about our team's development process. It was reprinted in the IEEE Tutorial on Software Maintenance.
